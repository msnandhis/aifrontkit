# Product editions and runtime independence

Community is a complete frontend foundation. It includes normalized runtime
contracts, accessible primitives, essential components, official adapters,
semantic tokens, public themes, the public registry, CLI, compatibility fixtures
and representative workflows.

Commercial editions add acceleration and organization features:

- Pro patterns, finished application templates and premium theme packs;
- protected source acquisition and updates;
- team registry namespaces, organization defaults and staged updates;
- Studio authoring, collaboration and review;
- onboarding, prioritized support and enterprise agreements.

Commercial access never changes the normalized interaction model. Official
adapters, accessibility fixes, security fixes and essential recovery behavior do
not move behind a paid entitlement.

## Downloaded source stays portable

Public source and lawfully acquired commercial source compile and run without an
AIFrontKit account. Customer applications do not call AIFrontKit billing,
entitlement or analytics services at runtime. Entitlements apply when protected
assets are acquired or updated.

The team registry uses the same public manifests and signed provenance model as
the public registry. It adds protected namespaces and organization policy rather
than a proprietary component format.

## Adoption measurement

AIFrontKit packages do not collect product analytics. Developer studies may use
an optional aggregate export after explicit consent. It contains cohort counts,
coarse completion buckets and fixed outcome categories. It cannot contain
prompts, messages, source code, files, URLs or participant identifiers.
